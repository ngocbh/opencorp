# agent-corp

Runs AI employees on your machine, on top of [Yian](https://github.com/ngocbh/yian). Early stage.

## Goals

- Yian flows hold each employee's traits and SOPs: repeatable work that improves over long runs.
- Flexible or one-off work runs in Codex, Claude Code, or OpenCode, unchanged. agent-corp lets
  an employee act as their user.
- agent-corp dogfoods Yian: whenever we can, work is automated with Yian YAML flows that people
  and agents share and reuse.
